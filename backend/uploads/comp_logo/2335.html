Nourish your taste buds and your health with Groundnut Oil

Nutritional Benefits

Groundnut oil is rich in monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats, which are beneficial for heart health and can help lower cholesterol levels.

Culinary Uses

Groundnut oil has a mild, nutty flavor and is commonly used in cooking and frying. It has a high smoke point, making it ideal for high-heat cooking methods.

Skincare and Beauty

Groundnut oil is known for its moisturizing properties and is often used in natural skincare and beauty products.

Sustainability and Fair trade

Many producers of groundnut oil prioritize sustainable and fair trade practices, ensuring that farmers and workers receive fair wages and that the environment is protected.
